## My work

### The customer's job

> When I have a question about my order, return, or warranty, I want a correct answer immediately, so I can stop worrying and not wait in a queue.

- Situation: a moment of uncertainty after a purchase.
- Motivation: a correct answer, now.
- Outcome: relief, and no queue.

### The agent's job

> When routine questions flood in all day, I want them handled without me, so I can spend my time on the complex cases that actually need a person.

- Situation: repetitive load.
- Motivation: the routine questions handled elsewhere.
- Outcome: skilled time spent where it matters.

### Reading

Neither job is "a chatbot." Both reduce to the same thing: **an instant, correct answer to a common question, drawn from knowledge Sunrise already has.** "Build a support bot" has become a problem, not a product, and a problem more than one kind of solution could serve.

_Note to self: caught myself about to write "chatbot" in the customer job. Stripped it. The no-tool rule is harder than it looks._
