### Reading

The ladder does not stop at the tempting symptoms:

- Not "support is understaffed" (hiring more agents treats the symptom).
- Not "we have no chatbot" (a tool, not a cause).

The wall appears only at **unreachable answers**. That is a reachability problem, and it is exactly what a deflection option addresses. The 5 Whys confirms the frame from a third direction.
